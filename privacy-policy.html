Last Updated: 12 November 2026

Europe Job Alerts ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what your rights are when you use our website and app. By using Europe Job Alerts, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or ask for your personal information.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, home address, CNIC / ID card number, passport number, photo, CV, or location.

When you use our Free Tools like CV Maker, Cover Letter Maker, or Salary Calculator, whatever you type stays only in your own mobile / browser. It is NOT sent to our server, because we have no server to store it.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not ask for your password. We do not track your exact location. We do not access your contacts, gallery, camera, or microphone, except when YOU yourself choose a photo for Passport Size Photo Maker. That photo also stays only in your mobile and is not uploaded to us.

3. Third-Party Links and Job Sources

Our app shows job listings collected from public third-party job websites, for example Arbeitnow, Jobicy, and company websites.

When you click on "Apply Karein", you will leave our app and go to that company's or that job website's page. That website has its own Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for what data that website collects from you. Please read their Privacy Policy before applying.

We do not own those jobs, we do not give jobs, and we do not guarantee that you will get a job or visa.

4. Cookies

Our website may use very basic cookies only to make the website work properly, for example to remember that the page loaded correctly. We do not use cookies to track you personally or to sell your data.

You can delete cookies from your browser settings at any time. The website will still work.

5. Ads

At the moment, our website does NOT show any third-party ads.

In future, if we show ads from Google AdMob or any other ad company, that company may use cookies and device information to show you relevant ads. That company will have its own Privacy Policy. We will update this page before we start showing ads.

We never sell your personal data to anyone, because we do not collect it.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is NOT made for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this app without your parent or guardian. If a parent thinks their child has given us any information by mistake, please contact us and we will help to remove it.

7. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is no risk of your data leaking from our side. Still, please never share your password, OTP, bank details, or passport copy with anyone who asks in the name of a job. A real company never asks for money before giving a job.

8. Your Rights

You have full control. You can stop using the app at any time. Since we do not store your data, there is nothing to delete from our side. If you used our Tools, you can clear your browser data to remove anything saved in your own mobile.

9. Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add a new feature or ads. Any change will be posted on this same page, with a new "Last Updated" date. Please check this page from time to time.
